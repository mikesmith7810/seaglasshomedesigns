**Hi, I'm Cathy, founder and creative lead of Sea Glass Home Designs.**

With a lifetime love of interiors born from my childhood in France — getting involved with property viewings and house renovations from a young age and being surrounded by the most inspiring classic architecture — I have gained extensive experience in renovating homes and staging our last property which sold on its viewing opening day, and am fortunate to have turned my passion for interior design and styling into my profession, offering both design and property staging services.

I started Sea Glass Home Designs from the belief that every home has the potential to feel like a sanctuary and my love of bringing out a home's best qualities, joining aesthetics with functionality to create a warm, balanced and nurturing environment. I am a firm believer that home is not just a look — it is first a feeling. I help homeowners transform their house into a home where they can relax and be themselves in a beautiful and practical personalised space, and create aspirational living for potential buyers through home staging.

With a keen eye for colour, light, and space, I work closely with you to understand your property and your goals to create a space that connects with homeowners and buyers on an emotional level.

**Why sea glass?** I see beauty and inspiration everywhere and living by the sea is no exception. Sea glass for me embodies an inherent connection to nature with its organic forms, and the timeless thrill and intrigue each unique piece of treasure brings.

Get in touch for a friendly, no-obligation conversation about how I can help create the same appeal in your space!
