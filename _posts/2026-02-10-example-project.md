This beautiful Victorian terrace had real character but had been on the market for several weeks without generating much interest. The owners asked me to come in and give it a fresh perspective.

The main living areas were decluttered and reorganised to make the rooms feel larger and more inviting. We introduced a soft, neutral palette with warm accents — think linen cushions, coastal textures, and carefully chosen accessories that let the original period features speak for themselves.

In the main bedroom, we created a calm and restful atmosphere with layered bedding, bedside lamps, and a simple piece of artwork that drew the eye to the beautiful original fireplace.

The property went under offer within ten days of re-listing.

**What we did:**
- Full living room and dining room restyling
- Master bedroom refresh
- Declutter and reorganise throughout
- Sourced and placed accessories and soft furnishings
