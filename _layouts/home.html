1. The Impact on Sale Price

Is it worth the money? The evidence says yes.

  • A strong return on investment — Every £1,000 spent on staging can return up to £10,000 in the final sale price, according to industry surveys from the Home Staging Association UK.
  • Sell for more — Staged homes consistently achieve higher sale prices — typically up to 10% more than unstaged properties. On a £300,000 home, that could mean tens of thousands of pounds more.
  • An affordable investment — Staging costs a small fraction of a property's guide price, making it one of the most cost-effective things you can do before going to market.
  • Avoid costly price reductions — Unstaged properties that sit on the market often face price reductions. Professional staging before listing can help you avoid that conversation entirely.

2. The Impact on Time on Market

Staging reduces the disruption of prolonged listings and helps sellers move on sooner.

  • Sell significantly faster — Staged properties can sell up to three times faster than unstaged ones, according to research from the Home Staging Association UK.
  • Reduce the carrying costs — Every extra week a property sits unsold costs money — mortgage payments, utilities, council tax. Less time on the market means real savings adding up.

3. The Psychology of the Buyer

This is why staging works — bridging the gap between a house and a home.

  • Buyers can picture themselves at home — The majority of buyers' agents say that staging makes it easier for clients to envision a property as their future home, particularly in empty spaces.
  • Stand out online and attract more viewings — Your first showing is online. A staged home stands out in search results, attracts more views and converts significantly more browsers into viewers.
  • Buyers stay longer at viewings — Most buyers spend more time in a staged home, giving them the space to connect emotionally and make a decision.

* Based on average data from the National Association of REALTORS® (NAR) and the Home Staging Association (HSA) UK. Actual results vary by property and current market conditions.
