## A Home That's Truly Yours

Great interior design isn't just about how a room looks — it's about how it feels to live in every day. At Sea Glass Home Designs, I work closely with you to create spaces that are beautiful, practical, and a genuine reflection of who you are.

From a single room refresh to a whole-home transformation, every project begins with understanding you — your tastes, your habits, and the atmosphere you want to come home to. The result is a design that feels effortless, considered, and completely personal.
