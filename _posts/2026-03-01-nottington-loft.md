This lovely 5-bedroom cottage in Weymouth, Dorset underwent a full interior design transformation as part of a loft conversion project.

The newly converted loft bedroom was styled for a teenage boy, with a warm and inviting colour scheme chosen to reflect his personality while making the most of the space and natural light the conversion created.

The result speaks for itself — the interior redesign added 20% to the value of the property, a clear example of how professional interior design and styling can deliver a significant return on investment.
