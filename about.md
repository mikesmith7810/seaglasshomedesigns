## Hello, I'm Cathy

I'm a professional home stager and interior stylist passionate about helping homeowners and estate agents present properties at their absolute best.

I started Sea Glass Home Styling because I believe every home has the potential to make buyers fall in love — it's simply about knowing how to show it. Whether that's rearranging what's already there, bringing in the right accessories, or transforming an empty property into somewhere people can truly picture their lives, I love the process of drawing out a home's best qualities.

With a keen eye for colour, light, and space, I work closely with you to understand your property and your goals — and then create a look that connects with buyers on an emotional level.

**I work with:**
- Homeowners preparing to sell
- Estate agents wanting to maximise the appeal of their listings
- Developers and landlords with vacant properties

Get in touch for a friendly, no-obligation conversation about how I can help.
