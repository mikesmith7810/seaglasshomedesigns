How It Works

01

Initial Consultation

We start with a conversation about your home, your lifestyle, and what you want to achieve. Whether you have a clear vision or need direction from scratch, I'll listen carefully and help shape ideas that are right for you.

02

Mood Board & Design Concept

I'll create a detailed mood board and design scheme for each room — bringing together colour palettes, textures, furniture styles, and finishes into a cohesive vision you can see and respond to before anything is purchased or changed.

03

Sourcing & Planning

Once the design is agreed, I'll source furniture, fabrics, lighting, and accessories that fit your budget and style. Every item is considered as part of the whole — nothing is chosen in isolation.
