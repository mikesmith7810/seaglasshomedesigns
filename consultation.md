  With the majority of buyers now searching for their new home online, your listing photos are the key to those first impressions that will either lead to viewings or people scrolling past. Not all homes require full staging to achieve the best results. If you are living in the home you are selling and want to improve your opportunities to attract viewings, a home staging consultation to guide you through strategic changes you can implement yourself can be very effective.

  Our Owner Occupied Consultation service is a hands-on, in-person consultation where we will spend up to two hours walking through your property with you, room by room, giving you practical tailored advice on decluttering, depersonalising, and presenting your property to appeal to the widest market.   It's the ideal starting point if you want professional guidance before going to market, or if your listing is not attracting viewings — without the cost of a full staging package. You'll leave with a clear picture of what changes will make the biggest difference to how your property presents to buyers.

  Many clients find that acting on Owner Occupied Consultation advice alone makes a significant difference to how their property performs online and at viewings.
