What's Included in Your Package

Everything you need to present your property at its very best — furniture, lighting, accessories and artwork brought together through professional styling to showcase your property's full potential and create a look that appeals to a wider market, delivered, styled and removed when the time comes.

  • An initial conversation — virtual or onsite
  • A bespoke staging plan tailored to your property style and local target market
  • Full staging set up and styling including delivery and assembly of furniture, accessories and lighting
  • A minimum of 8 weeks of aspirational presentation and rental of all furniture and accessories
  • De-staging and removal of all furniture, accessories and lighting once the rental period is over

Why Staging Works

The numbers speak for themselves — staged properties sell faster, attract stronger offers and stand out in a crowded market.

  • Stand out online — Professional staging transforms your photography, driving more clicks and converting browsers into viewers.
  • Sell faster — Staged properties can sell up to three times quicker than empty equivalents.
  • Achieve stronger prices — Staged homes consistently achieve higher sale prices, often significantly above unstaged comparables.
  • Reduce holding costs — Less time on the market means real savings on mortgage payments, utilities and council tax.
